# Map time, closed roads and stops

## Quick guide

1. **Map is live.** The compact header shows **Live map**, current PKT time and date. To inspect another instant, open **Plan ahead** from the side/bottom navigation, enter **Date & time · Pakistan**, then **Show map**. Its header clearly says **Planned map**. **Map** or **Return to live map** restores current time; Plan ahead remembers the last applied date.
2. **Red roads are closed at the displayed time.** Yellow pointers mark upcoming closures. Hover or keyboard-focus a future pointer to temporarily highlight its affected road sections in yellow; leaving removes that preview. Click/tap pins the preview and opens the schedule, with another tap, an outside click or Escape to dismiss it. Expired events are hidden. Active markers open their schedule and editing controls.
3. **Blue routes are validated travel options.** A conflicting original route is pale red and labelled **Blocked**. It is not selectable for navigation.
4. Startup has empty **A / Starting point** and **B / Destination**, no pins and no route. Choose both to enable **Find routes**. **Add stop** adds an intermediate location. Search or pick on the map, reorder with arrows, remove with the trash button, or reverse the entire journey. Up to five stops are supported.
5. Admin **Demo closures** provides ten synthetic examples. **Add defaults** enables the original six; four focused examples have **Try route** for ordinary public-street detours. **Clear demos** leaves ordinary reports alone. A demo route opens the live Map. Admin route results also expose **Block a point on this route**. Guests/ordinary users cannot mutate demo data.
6. Zoom and pan worldwide. The local detailed graph covers central Islamabad; other Pakistan journeys use validated online routing. Worldwide routing is not implemented. [Provider limits](ROUTING.md).

**Closures** remains a primary tab for everyone; adding restrictions starts there. **Activity** appears after sign-in and shows totals/history without a manual recorder. Navigation captures meaningful foreground trips only with personal-data consent. The tutorial starts after registration or explicit guest entry, not ordinary login/resumed guest reload. Everyone can replay it from Help or Profile. Seven separately seeded [month-long local test closures](LOCAL_TEST_CLOSURES.md) are explicitly synthetic and do not replace the admin demo catalogue.

## Time and route behavior

The header also shows a compact **Islamabad-only hourly weather** icon and temperature. Live map uses its current hour; Plan ahead selects the exact displayed hour from the shared forecast. Click/tap the weather for condition, timestamp and source details. Minute clock ticks do not fetch weather, and weather never triggers route/map reloads. Unavailable forecast dates remain unavailable rather than reusing another day's values. These are model estimates; [coverage, cache and source details](WEATHER.md).

There is one active instant for the displayed workspace. Only Plan ahead uses the remembered selected date; every other section, including Closures/Profile/Admin/Alerts and Navigation, uses the live clock. A preview is a projection over **currently known published restrictions**, not a reconstruction of archived historical database state. Past dates may be selected without claiming historical completeness.

The live clock refreshes roughly once a minute, on focus and at known closure start/end boundaries. A planned selection stays fixed during clock ticks. The date input is an unapplied draft until **Show map**, which reads the native submitted field and validates its PKT calendar value. Header status is never a date-picker button. Switching live/planned contexts or editing endpoints clears old route geometry and pending sequences; delayed results must match both the request and current time context. Clock/revision changes for the same journey now refresh quietly: the last validated blue routes, labels and route cards remain visible/inspectable until the complete replacement arrives. There is no grey Updating state for that passive refresh. **Navigate** waits for fresh validation, and active guidance still pauses on a changed closure revision. Refresh failures retain the display with an explicit error and do not unlock stale navigation. Automatic requests run only in the planners with an owned loaded state; sign-out cannot replan private endpoints anonymously. Camera/valid selection are preserved, and opening settings does not start hidden replanning.

Choosing an earlier/later departure or checking a saved commute opens **Plan ahead** and recalculates at that occurrence. A blocked original remains an inspectable comparison in the same time context. Planned route results offer **View live route**, which returns to Map and obtains a current-time plan; only then is **Navigate** available. A future preview never directly starts GPS. Departure alternatives are never overlaid as if they belonged to the live snapshot.

Map red state uses exact `[startsAt, endsAt)` membership, without the routing margin. Routing evaluates when the vehicle reaches each polygon, with the existing 30-second margin. A road which closes after the trip has passed can remain usable; a road reopening before arrival can also be usable. These time-dependent cases are different from a permanent road exclusion. Future closures intersecting the original route within 24 hours appear as a short upcoming notice, explicitly labelled as applying to the original route.

Foreground navigation keeps its accepted in-progress route while the map clock advances. It does not silently replace that route every minute. Existing stale-closure/GPS guards still apply; continuous time-dependent resimulation during GPS travel remains future work.

## Road painting

`GET /api/closures/roads?at=...` returns the affected portions of actual graph edges for published closures active at that instant. The optional **`includeUpcoming=true`** additionally returns known published future geometry for inspection. This does not activate those closures in routing. The map includes upcoming IDs/revisions in its geometry-cache key, still suppresses stale responses, paints only active roads red, and puts only the currently inspected scheduled event in the yellow layer. Active red coating takes precedence where sections overlap. Expired, cancelled and draft events are excluded.

Rendering and routing share one cached graph/polygon intersection calculation. Red endpoints are interpolated at the actual polygon boundary rather than extended to the full OSM edge. Holes split the painted line; tangent-only contacts do not paint a false arm. Reverse directed copies are deduplicated, and flat red end caps avoid extending past the reported endpoints. Ordinary browsing has no surrounding polygon outline/fill; the editor still previews its authoring geometry. Unchanged road geometry is cached to avoid delayed or disappearing lines on clock updates. See [performance evidence](PERFORMANCE.md).

Closure symbols retain MapLibre's own positioning/anchor classes and transforms across state updates. Their geographic position uses the midpoint of the longest affected road section, with a valid line drawing or area-center fallback only when matched geometry is unavailable. They do not receive zoom-dependent pixel offsets. Repeated preview renders avoid replacing unchanged marker text or resetting unchanged coordinates. Escape/second-tap dismissal remains latched while the same pointer/focus stays on a marker; genuinely leaving and returning can show it again. Hover has keyboard-focus and click/tap equivalents.

The red line represents the affected portion of a road, not the entire road sharing its name. Draw the whole blocked stretch as a road corridor to close a longer stretch. A two-dimensional polygon may also cover a nearby carriageway, ramp or flyover; lane-specific and direction-specific closure editing remain future work. No GPS shortcut, missing road or visual crossing is invented as a connection. Routing still treats uncertain boundary contact conservatively; trimming paint does not relax route validation. A time-dependent route can use a road after its announced reopening, even if the departure snapshot still paints it red.

## Ordered stops

`PlanRequest.waypoints?: Place[]` and `CommuteDraft.waypoints?: Place[]` accept at most five ordered intermediate places. Responses include `snappedWaypoints` when stops exist. One continuous A* state carries the incoming edge and next-stop progress. This preserves one-way/turn restrictions at a stop and cumulative arrival time for later-leg closures. Stops add no dwell time. The whole path is revalidated, including alternative and departure options. Unsupported or disconnected stops return unavailable.

Public mapped residential/service streets can be used as detours. Private, access-restricted, disconnected and missing roads remain excluded. The search is bounded and does not prove every possible real-world alternative has been found.

Saved journeys preserve stops and check them for notifications. Route preview works with stops. Foreground GPS guidance now tracks remaining stops and automatically reroutes after sustained deviation. See [bilingual navigation](BILINGUAL_NAVIGATION_AND_CLOUD.md) for evidence requirements, restart behavior and limitations.
