# CDA transit verification

Checked **18 October 2026 (Asia/Karachi)** against CDA's own public website, not a third-party copy. The machine-readable evidence is [cda-verification.json](../../assets/transit/cda-verification.json), generated by [verify-cda-transit.py](../../scripts/verify-cda-transit.py). This verifies **what the official sources publish**. It does not certify every route/stop as current or authorize a claim that every bus is covered.

## Findings that affect the app

1. CDA's statement dated **14 October**, published **15 October 2026**, reports **160 electric buses operating on 21 routes**. It does not list the 21 route codes, stops or coordinates. An aggregate operating count cannot certify all entries from an older route table. [Official statement](https://www.cda.gov.pk/cdaImagesGallery/cda-commitment-for-provision-of-modern-environment-friendly-electric-bus-service-to-address-transpiration-needs-of-citizen-in-islamabad).
2. The current [CDA transit portal](https://www.cda.gov.pk/cdaTransitMap) publishes **22 route entries**, **42 directional timetable PDFs**, and a disabled detail link for **FR-04B**. All 42 downloaded PDFs were created **16 July 2025**. None supplies stop coordinates, georeferenced route geometry or current service-validity dates.
3. All 42 PDFs were parsed across **1,244 pages / 2,823 listed trips**. Their parsed trip counts agree with their stated totals, and each direction has one stable named-stop sequence. These are published historical timetable facts; they are not imported as a current operating calendar.
4. **FR-05 and ST-01** are present in the portal but absent from the reviewed mapped dataset. They can be exposed as dated, unmapped references. Their names alone do not justify map pins, straight-line pseudo-routes or scheduled journeys.
5. Existing source data and official PDFs differ substantially. **Keep the newer mapped data and show the disagreement**; do not replace geometry automatically because a document is hosted on an official domain.

The portal's **FR-05** heading and PDF long name say *Golara Morh to Taxila*, while its actual named sequence begins **NUST Metro Station**, passes Police Foundation / G-10 / Sumbal Road / Khayaban-e-Iqbal / NPF / Islamabad Gardens / E-11 / IESCO, and ends **D-12**. This internal disagreement must be visible in a reference panel. It is not a reliable basis for a newly mapped Golra–Taxila service. [Forward PDF](https://www.cda.gov.pk/Assets/metro_transit_route/FR-05_Forward.pdf), [backward PDF](https://www.cda.gov.pk/Assets/metro_transit_route/FR-05_Backward.pdf).

**FR-11** illustrates why recency matters: the current portal heading says I-16, the July PDF long name ends at Rafiullah Shaheed Chowk, and the mapped source contains 20/19 stops to I-16/4. A title is not proof the PDF's short pattern covers the full current line. [Official PDF](https://www.cda.gov.pk/Assets/metro_transit_route/FR-11_Forward.pdf).

**FR-01 Forward** has another source conflict: its title says *Khana Pul to NUST*, but its 17-stop sequence starts at NUST Metro Station and ends at MCI Model School. Visual inspection of both the first and last pages confirms that the final listed trip also ends at MCI Model School at 22:45; this is not a lost page continuation in extraction. The opposite-direction PDF contains 26 stops. [Official forward PDF](https://www.cda.gov.pk/Assets/metro_transit_route/FR-01_Forward.pdf).

Two lines have close textual agreement without resolving coordinate or currency questions. **FR-08C** retains all 23 stops in the same order in each direction, with `School Board Stop` / `School Board` and `Tamma Stop` / `Tamma` as the only positional label differences. **Blue** matches every forward label exactly; its 17-stop backward sequence differs only at `PIMS Metro Station` / `PIMS Metro`. These comparisons do not use a guessed alias table. A strict literal-name comparison should label these remaining suffix differences explicitly rather than claim every label is identical.

## Additional references, current operation and planned services

The portal's downloadable [V-08 network diagram](https://www.cda.gov.pk/Assets/pdf/Transit-Map-Islamabad-V-08.pdf) was created **24 October 2025** according to its PDF metadata. It includes a future-route legend and dashed corridors, including references such as FR-02 and FR-03B. It also mentions FR-08B, which has no directional timetable link in the table. A diagram entry is recorded separately from a current operational timetable.

The same diagram identifies ST-01/ST-02 as **Saturday/Sunday special trips** and the Diplomatic Enclave shuttle as Monday–Friday. It is schematic, lacks coordinates and depicts proposed extensions. Pixel tracing would create false geographic precision. First-page visual inspection confirmed the future legend, route key and special-trip notes; timetable first pages for FR-01, FR-05, FR-11, ST-01 and Blue, plus the FR-01 final page, were also rendered and compared with extracted text.

**EX-16, FR-08B and FR-17** are in the mapped source but absent from the portal's timetable table. Orange/airport services are drawn in the diagram but likewise have no linked directional PDFs there. These gaps do not prove cancellation. No public official route-specific document was established here that updates all these patterns to October 2026.

CDA's **23 February 2026** announcement independently reports 21 operating electric routes and Google integration, but again does not provide an openly downloadable GTFS/stop-coordinate link. [Official announcement](https://www.cda.gov.pk/cdaImagesGallery/islamabad-mohsin-naqvi-inaugurates-new-electric-buses-depot-and-charging-facility).

The September 2026 [double-decker sightseeing announcement](https://www.cda.gov.pk/cdaImagesGallery/cda-to-introduce-electric-double-decker-buses-to-facilitate-citizens-and-tourists-in-sightseeing-of-beautiful-capital-of-pakistan) describes an introduction, and the January 2026 [tram announcement](https://www.cda.gov.pk/public/cdaImagesGallery/cda-initiates-electric-tram-and-e-bus-project-to-promote-green-transport-in-islamabad) describes a feasibility study. Neither is converted into a confirmed available service.

## Machine-readable and vendor checks

- The CDA transit page's HTML links only PDFs for transit details. Its iframe is the same PDF, not a hidden interactive geographic map. No GTFS, KML, GeoJSON, stop-coordinate table or transit API link was found in that page.
- [CMTA's public site](https://cmta.pk/) and its documented WordPress public page index link older schematic PDFs and Google Maps journey planning. This is not a documented bulk-coordinate feed. WordPress page modification dates do not establish route-data currency.
- The official CDA Android listing identifies its [CDA Metro App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kentkart.cdamobile), and [Kentkart's own Pakistan reference](https://www.kentkart.com/pakistan-kentkart-references/) confirms vendor work in Islamabad. Kentkart's public general city endpoint, `https://service.kentkart.com/rl1/api/v2.0/city`, returned **27 Turkish cities**, with no Islamabad region. No hidden region was guessed, APK reverse engineering performed, account endpoint accessed, token extracted or login bypass attempted.
- Public app functionality and Google Maps integration do not themselves grant redistribution rights or disclose a reusable feed. The public source page or a data-sharing agreement must establish that separately.

## Reproduce and use the evidence

```powershell
python scripts/verify-cda-transit.py --fetch
```

Raw HTML/PDFs, rendered images and extracted caches remain in ignored `.local-tools/transit-official/`. The collector accepts CDA domains, caps source size, limits concurrent downloads to four and hashes every PDF. It preserves PDF text-flow order because long stop names overlap the arrival-time column spatially; simple spatial extraction can lose a stop. Cached extraction is keyed by the raw hash and extraction version.

`comparedDatasetVersion` records the data inspected when the manifest was generated. Consumers can use `committedRouteId`, `committedStopSequences`, `committedUnplacedStopNames`, directional `stopSequences`, `parseIssues`, and per-document dates/URLs to create **consistent / differences / reference-only / not-established** labels. Matching names or counts verifies a textual reference, **never coordinates or current operation**. Rebinding after a dataset change requires a fresh comparison.

No source PDF, account information, GPS trace or credential belongs in Git. The normalized evidence records public facts with attribution and source links; **no explicit open redistribution licence was found for the official source PDFs**. They are linked, not bundled. This audit adds no live API dependency, bus tracking, inferred coordinate, current fare, automatic service cancellation or production deployment.
