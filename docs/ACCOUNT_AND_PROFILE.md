# Accounts and profile — 17 October

These changes are localhost work on `codex/feature-development`. The core app already has a Firebase deployment; publishing this feature branch does not release these screens or activate cloud scheduling. The deployment developer handles integration and release. No local database, GPS history or credentials are migrated.

## Using the screens

1. **Start:** the left column shows the circular logo, English **Manzil**, one prominent **Continue as guest**, and email/password sign-in with a sign-in icon. The redundant guest X is removed. **Create account** and **Forgot password?** open their own forms; guest access remains available. The right-hand map is a lightweight static illustration of a closed road and a detour, not a live closure feed. The matte-green backdrop has original white country-outline/road-scan, travelling-pin detour and detailed roadside bus/walker/sign scenes, with a small card shadow, desktop pause/play, hidden-tab pause and static phone/reduced-motion scenery. Narrow screens prioritize the form. Automatic onboarding follows registration or explicit guest entry only; ordinary login and resumed guest reload skip it. Local registration/reset lifecycle is tested; production inbox delivery is a separate check. [Artwork/auth evidence and limits](ENTRY_SCREEN.md), [tour behavior](DEV_ACCOUNTS_AND_TOUR.md).
2. **Header:** the avatar opens **User profile**. **Dark** is the new/missing app preference, independently of the **Night** map default. Saved Light/Dark/System and map styles remain intact; System continues to follow the operating system. A sun/moon button switches and saves the effective light/dark appearance. Guests retain appearance locally in Firebase mode. Light uses neutral-grey outer panels and pale-blue cards with bordered green identity/icon tiles; Dark keeps its existing palette. The styled **English / اردو** menu supports arrow keys, Home/End, Enter, Escape, Tab and outside dismissal; notifications remain separate. Narrow screens retain the circular logo and compact live clock, leaving room for accessible controls.
3. **Map:** new accounts, new guest preferences and missing styles start with **Night**. Existing deliberate style selections remain saved. Legacy Streets/High contrast IDs render as Classic; Minimal has darker roads and clearer labels. **My location** requests one position only after a click and recentres to zoom 15 without changing endpoints, stops, route or planned time; it neither starts a watcher nor records a trip. Choose a start separately through the planner's map picker. **Fit journey** remains; create restrictions through **Closures → Add closure**.
4. **Profile:** the overview contains identity and compact settings rows with current values. The guest sign-in action has its own gap below the identity row. **Edit** opens name, optional photo, searchable Pakistan city and neighbourhood in **Personal information**, beside the overview. The city catalogue loads on opening its field, renders a capped shortlist and retains an unmatched previously saved city; see [data scope and attribution](PAKISTAN_CITIES.md). The photo is prepared locally as a draft. **Save profile** persists the whole edit; **Cancel**, Close, Escape or outside dismissal discards an unsaved draft. First Escape closes an open city list; a subsequent Escape closes settings. Changing city/area does not request location access.
5. **Personal trip data:** the overview shows On/Off directly; selecting the row opens its permission panel. It starts Off. New registrations receive **Allow personal trip data / Not now** before the tour. Either choice is remembered; existing accounts are not forced through a new prompt. Collection and usual-route learning can be stopped later in Profile.
6. **Personal statistics:** opens monthly distance, time, trip count and estimated fuel spending in the shared secondary panel. **Trip history** opens the same panel with summaries, explicit View/Export and deletion controls; it never starts another recorder. Activity remains the separate primary destination for explicit trip recording and history.
7. **Settings:** **Your vehicle**, **Fuel prices**, **Appearance & language** and **Journey alerts** all use the same panel size, position, heading, close control and focus handling, also shared with identity, trip data, statistics and history. Click outside any of these panels to close it; the click is consumed rather than also activating the map or another control. Repeating the active setting action toggles it closed. Interacting inside, including the city list, does not close it, and a drag begun inside does not become an outside dismissal. X/Escape and focus return remain available. Vehicle details have their own **Save vehicle** action; dismissal cancels an unsaved draft. Browser notification permission remains a separate explicit action; learning requires trip-data permission. On smaller screens the panel becomes a centered, scrollable dialog. Its close control stays visible while long content scrolls.
8. **Populated preview:** choose **User 123** under development accounts to see fictional settings and monthly history. Its badges identify sample data, and GPS collection stays Off. See [credentials, contents and safe seeding](SAMPLE_ACCOUNT.md).

## Fuel prices and monthly spending

The Fuel prices panel shows the latest applicable dated petrol/diesel reference, effective date, source link, last check and cached/unavailable state. The free public [PSO POL archive](https://psopk.com/fuel-prices/pol/archives) supplies the existing collector; [PSO's fuel-price page](https://psopk.com/en/fuels/fuel-prices) links its price information. No social-media subscription or Champ extraction is needed for this source.

The running local worker checks **once per Pakistan date**. A daily check does not mean prices change every day. Published effective dates are kept separately from retrieval dates; PSO reference prices exclude outlet freight. There is no assumed government JSON API or hardcoded live price. Production scheduling remains separate release work. See [fuel contracts and coverage](FUEL_COSTS.md).

Vehicle type, optional name, petrol/diesel and user-entered **km/litre** are private preferences. Saving them does not grant GPS consent. Routes retain the car-road access/time model even for motorcycle fuel estimates. Recorded trips keep immutable vehicle/rate snapshots; unknown or uncovered periods remain unpriced. Monthly totals identify missing prices and partial retained-history coverage.

## Developer boundaries

- `AccountPanel` uses the existing accessible modal/focus handling. `public/manzil-route-preview.svg` is repo-native illustrative artwork, with no map/network dependency. Development-account shortcuts stay collapsed and localhost-development-only; normal screens omit emulator implementation notices.
- Profile editing stages photo/name/location locally. Photo processing retains image validation/re-encoding and account-change guards; a late result for another UID cannot become the current draft. Form errors keep the draft available for retry.
- `ProfileSettingsDialog` is the shared accessible wrapper. Outside dismissal is opt-in here; account, consent and tour dialogs retain their own existing behavior. The parent controls one open section at a time; view/UID changes clear it. App snapshots are exposed only to their matching account. Late panel errors/photo results do not leak into another opened section. Guest settings retain only appearance/language and public fuel prices.
- `TripHistory` is shared with Activity, without any GPS watcher. Viewing an available saved trace takes display precedence over a paused recording draft while preserving that draft; dismissing the history overlay restores the draft. Summary-only demo trips have no geometry to View/Export. No generated GPS traces are seeded.
- `MonthlyStatistics` reads only `GET /api/trips/statistics`, with loading, timeout, retry and partial/unpriced states. Opening the dialog never starts a GPS watcher, requests browser location or loads raw trip traces. Activity reuses the same monthly presentation; backend `week`, `month` and `all` fields remain available for compatibility.
- Optional `LocalProfile.travelDataChoiceMade` records acknowledgement, **not collection consent**. Only successful registration marks that UID pending in memory/session storage; the API stores the acknowledged choice. Allow sets collection and inference true; Not now sets both false. Dismissing the consent dialog follows Not now. Failed writes remain retryable and do not claim the preference changed. See [personal-data lifecycle](PERSONAL_TRAVEL_DATA.md) and [API](API.md).
- Route, closure/time/access validation, private UID storage, explicit unavailable states and retention rules are unchanged. Gemini, cloud speech activation, background GPS and reliable closed-app push are not activated by these UI changes.

Automated verification covers shared-panel behavior, staged photo Save/Cancel, late-photo UID isolation, permission toggles, monthly retained-history totals, unavailable/partial totals, explicit history actions and no extra GPS watcher. Final integrated counts and browser evidence belong in [implementation status](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md).
