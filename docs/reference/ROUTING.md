# Driving routing

Manzil separates basemap display, road selection and driving search. MapLibre/OpenFreeMap displays the map; the bundled OSM graph drives local searches, OSRM supplies candidates elsewhere in Pakistan, and bounded public OSM queries supply closure-editor geometry outside the offline extract. Drawing on the map never adds an edge to a routing graph.

| Concern | Coverage and provider |
| --- | --- |
| Driving/search endpoints | Pakistan coverage guard; local graph in central Islamabad, OSRM/Photon elsewhere |
| Closure road selection | No Islamabad city gate; offline fast path plus bounded on-demand OSM road area |
| Local public transport | Reviewed imported Islamabad/Rawalpindi network; separate [transit planner](TRANSIT_ROUTING.md) |
| Live traffic | Not implemented; duration uses road-speed assumptions/source timing |

Every candidate is revalidated for closure time/access and revision freshness. Private/pending reports and unapproved agent proposals never block a public journey. Road or bus proposals from Gemini always require administrator approval. See [closure geometry](../guides/CLOSURE_WORKFLOW.md), [road provider budgets/privacy](ROAD_COVERAGE.md) and [agent review](../operations/AGENT_MAP_REVIEW.md).

The foreground navigator can replan after sustained GPS deviation, closure revisions or substantial delay while preserving unvisited stops. Its hardware/speech/accuracy limits are in [navigation](../guides/BILINGUAL_NAVIGATION_AND_CLOUD.md). This reference concerns route validity rather than a guarantee of lane-aware navigation or current physical road access.

## Road sections and blocked comparisons

The closure editor's **Road closure** choice takes two clicks and follows the actual connected public-road geometry between them, including bends and split OSM ways. `POST /roads/section` accepts `{from:[longitude,latitude],to:[longitude,latitude]}` and returns `{drawing,roadNames,distanceMeters}`. The drawing remains a normal `kind:"line"` contract with a fixed 14 m full corridor width; it does not change base tiles or add graph edges. The editor uses the road name as a starting title. Separate roads and separate physical barriers need separate reports.

Selection uses accepted car-road segments from the bundled graph or a bounded on-demand Overpass graph, exact projected endpoints within 45 m, same-name connectivity (same-way identity for unnamed generic roads), a 6 km/400-vertex maximum, and bounded path search. Nearby candidates resolve ambiguous junction/carriageway clicks; no connection is invented at a visual crossing or along a private excluded road. Selection is geometric and may run either direction on a one-way segment because closure polygons have no direction field; this is **not** a driving itinerary or a relaxation of routing one-way/turn checks. External road-data failures return **503 unavailable**; unsuitable/disconnected/oversized sections return **422**. The simplified editor has Road closure and Blockade choices. It acquires missing editor road geometry from Overpass, independently of OSRM driving routes. Changing tools, editing the drawing or leaving the editor cancels a pending selection so its response cannot overwrite newer work.

Blocked originals are **inspectable comparisons**, with their ordinary unblocked time/distance, hypothetical arrival and affected closure details. Clicking the pale-red exclusive geometry, its **Blocked · N min** badge or its comparison card never makes that route eligible for navigation. Blue routes remain separately selected and validated. Shared open parts of the original/clear routes are removed from the pale-red display, including partial collinear overlaps with differently split nodes. The badge anchors only to exclusive original geometry, away from blue segments; it is omitted at zooms where a clear position is unavailable, while the card remains usable. Bright-red actual closure strokes are unchanged.

Normal planning requests **up to three** genuine routes. When fewer choices validate, the result says how many were found and discloses similar corridors, closures/search limits or the online provider's limited alternatives. Returning over a node on a later required-stop leg is not itself a fabricated-route loop. Loops within an individual leg remain excluded; ordered stops use one continuous search preserving incoming-turn state and traversal-time validation.

Focused regression coverage includes two-click bend/split-way geometry, private/different/disconnected road rejection, exact section clipping, explicit coverage errors, and a publishable selection over the actual Park Road fixture; exclusive blocked-overlay clipping/crossing separation; required-stop return alternatives; and comparison-only blocked cards without navigation eligibility. See `tests/road-selection.test.ts`, `tests/map-overlays.test.ts`, `tests/routing-alternatives.test.ts` and `tests/route-results.test.tsx`.

## Pakistan coverage and online providers

Origins, destinations and up to five ordered stops may be anywhere within the bundled Pakistan coverage boundary, including Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Gilgit and Muzaffarabad. There is no Islamabad endpoint gate at the public planner. The country guard runs before routing, so arbitrary foreign map pins or typed coordinates are rejected without an online route request. A simplified mapped boundary can be imperfect at a border/coast; this is an application coverage check, not a legal boundary statement.

- **Inside the imported Islamabad extent:** keep the existing local engine, closure detours, access/turn checks and local graph. A blocked local plan never falls back to a closure-unaware provider.
- **Elsewhere in Pakistan:** request real OSM car routes from the configurable OSRM-compatible endpoint. `OSRM_BASE_URL` defaults to the public `https://router.project-osrm.org` demo; no API key is required for this localhost prototype. Disconnected endpoints, excessive snapping, provider errors and unverifiable geometry/timing return explicit unavailable results.
- **Search:** retain local landmarks/road names and add Photon (`PHOTON_BASE_URL`, default `https://photon.komoot.io`) with `countrycode=PK`, an independent country-code response filter and a Pakistan polygon check for returned coordinates. Coordinate entry works even when online search fails.
- **Sector names:** complete sector forms such as `I8`, `I 8` and `I-8` share the same canonical `I-8` local/provider query and ten-minute cache entry; `F8/2` similarly becomes `F-8/2`. This changes punctuation only, not a place's coordinates or city. Uncertain text such as `IA8` is not silently rewritten to another destination. Photon can return fuzzy but irrelevant matches: inspect the displayed place/city, or use an exact name such as `I-8 Islamabad`, a map pin or coordinates. Search is OSM/Photon, with different coverage/ranking from Google Places; no Google search service is activated.
- **Google Places:** not integrated; named search uses OSM/Photon. Any replacement provider needs a separate review of its map-display, storage, billing and attribution terms.
- **Closure validation:** inspect each returned candidate against every published closure, preserving ordered-stop elapsed time and the 30-second safety margin. OSRM annotation durations exclude turn costs, so conservative per-leg timing bounds cover uncertain turn-cost placement. Unknown/malformed timing is rejected. Returned alternatives are filtered; OSRM cannot accept the app's arbitrary closure polygons, so it may supply no usable detour even when another road exists. The service's OSM access/turn profile is separate from local import rules and is not independently re-created by Manzil.
- **Caching and fair use:** bounded short-lived in-memory geometry/search caches, concurrent-request deduplication, bounded queues, timeouts and at most one remote request start per second. Cached geometry is revalidated against current closures, revisions and the selected departure. Responses arriving after a closure revision are rechecked before delivery/alerts.
- **Connectivity/privacy:** online search sends query text to Photon; online planning sends endpoint/stop coordinates to OSRM. Saved-commute checks and explicitly enabled usual-journey checks outside the local graph also send saved or representative endpoints/stops. Any configured scheduled commute checks have the same provider boundary; production commute scheduling is a separate operational setting. Raw trace arrays are never sent to these routing/search providers. Personal records stay in local SQLite, local Firestore Emulator, or private hosted Firestore according to mode. The services have no availability or live-traffic guarantee. Use compatible self-hosted/managed endpoints before heavier use. Defaults follow the providers' [OSRM demo policy](https://github.com/Project-OSRM/osrm-backend/wiki/Demo-server) and [Photon demo guidance](https://github.com/komoot/photon#demo-server).
- **Map coating:** in-coverage road intervals clip to actual graph-road intersections. Typed contained road/barrier lines retain their full selection beyond/across the offline boundary. Road intervals there carry `unverifiedDrawing: true`, because a type field alone is not persisted proof of mapped-road selection; blockades retain their separately validated transverse stroke. Legacy contained line drawings wholly outside coverage can also display as unverified. Point/polygon-only or uncontained drawings gain no invented road line. Stored polygons and selected-time/moderation rules continue to govern routing.
- **Tolls/checkposts:** the road route may physically pass mapped toll plazas/checkposts, but Manzil currently does not list them, quote toll charges, estimate queues or claim current checkpoint coverage. Route results state this explicitly. Existing OSM `toll_booth` passability is not a dedicated POI/cost dataset.

## What the local router enforces

- Actual OSM node connectivity, including separated bridge/tunnel geometry where mapped correctly; no connection is invented at a visual crossing.
- Directed one-way and roundabout edges, explicit car/vehicle/access restrictions, and a conservative supported public-road profile. Footways, construction and private/destination-only roads are excluded. Explicit higher-specificity car access takes precedence.
- Node-based `no_*` and `only_*` OSM turn restrictions, including prohibited U-turns. Unsupported via-way sequences conservatively exclude their via roads. Conditional access/one-way and reversible/alternating roads are excluded; conditional turn restrictions are applied continuously. This can remove usable routes.
- A* search using estimated road-class speeds, posted-speed caps, rough-surface caps and small junction delays. These are ordinary estimates, not measured traffic speeds or a turn-by-turn navigation guarantee.
- Endpoint snapping to actual road nodes within 220 metres. The response returns snapped coordinates and discloses the distance. Access from the original pin to that point is not routed; choose the correct carriageway on the map.

### Streets are part of ordinary routing

Public residential, living-street and permitted service roads participate in every local search; no street-detour mode, forced waypoint or UI opt-in is needed. Routes optimize ordinary estimated driving time, including the lower street speeds and junction delays. A shorter street is therefore chosen when it saves estimated time; a longer faster main road may still win. Published closures exclude affected traversals through the same ordinary connected search. Private/destination-only roads, footways and other excluded access classes remain unavailable.

The [demo catalogue](../engineering/DEMOS.md) supplies optional endpoint pairs and synthetic closures on Park Road, Rohtas Road, Tipu Sultan Road and Sumbal Road. `tests/routing-public-streets.test.ts` checks original directed OSM edges and residential tags for all four (489 m, 361 m, 1,042 m and 396 m of residential detour respectively), cancellation/reopening, travel-time ranking and private/footway exclusion. These are real-graph local test results, not current traffic advice. National OSRM requests use the provider's car profile; candidate filtering there cannot force an arbitrary street detour around Manzil polygons.

## Scheduled closures in the local router

Ordered intermediate stops are supported through `waypoints?: Place[]` (maximum five). The continuous search retains the incoming edge across each stop, so a prohibited turn is not bypassed by splitting the trip into independent legs. It carries elapsed travel time across the whole journey, adds no stop dwell time, and returns snapped stop coordinates. Saved commute checks preserve the same ordered stops. See [map time and stops](../guides/MAP_TIME_AND_STOPS.md).

Only published closures participate; cancelled and draft events do not block roads. The router intersects complete edge segments with the reviewed polygon, interpolates entry/exit along each edge and checks the traversal window against `[start,end)`, with a disclosed 30-second margin before entry and after exit. It checks every traversal, not just the trip departure. Unknown end remains open-ended. Invalid published schedules return an unavailable result pending review.

The baseline is retained for comparison even when blocked. A blocked baseline is never returned as a validated option. Conflicting edges are excluded for another connected route search, up to eight iterations, followed by complete revalidation. Optional departure comparison samples changes of 10, 20, 30, 45 and 60 minutes in each direction; past departures are not offered for a current/future trip. Departure changes are extra schedule choices, separate from the same-departure routes below. Each candidate is checked independently. Available choices are the best found among a bounded set, not a proof of global temporal optimality: a longer path arriving at a reopening later may be missed by fixed query exclusions.

## Local distinct routes at the same departure

The planner returns **up to three useful connected driving routes for exactly the selected departure**, ordered by original estimated driving time. The first has kind `direct` or `detour`; additional choices have kind `alternative`, unique IDs and a distinguishing road label where available. Fewer than three is an honest result when there is only one valid corridor, the alternatives are too similar, a route is excessively indirect, or the bounded search cannot validate more. The app does not invent extra choices.

Generation tries up to eight combinations of interior road-corridor exclusions and nonnegative overlap penalties. Both directions of a physical segment receive the generation constraint, to discourage a U-turn around the same corridor. **Artificial search penalties never change reported time:** after choosing geometry, the engine reconstructs every edge entry/exit, step time, total duration and arrival from original road speeds and junction delays, then validates all published closure intervals again. A closure on a candidate-only corridor is checked even if the baseline is unaffected.

An alternative must pass all of these product heuristics against accepted choices:

- No repeated-node loop introduced solely to manufacture a different route.
- At most 82% shared physical edge distance, measured against the shorter route.
- At least 120 m or 12% of the shorter route (whichever is larger) more than 80 m from the other route. A nearby parallel carriageway or tiny local detour therefore does not count as a separate route.
- Duration at most 1.5 times the first validated choice plus 60 seconds; distance at most 1.65 times that choice plus 250 metres. These are usefulness limits, not a claim that a longer real route is impossible.

The whole request shares a maximum of 32 A* searches and a three-second computation deadline, checked during closure geometry work and route expansion. Same-departure choices take priority over optional departure comparisons. If a limit is reached, only fully validated choices are retained and a planning note discloses the incomplete search. Static ordinary-speed baseline geometry can be reused at a different departure, but its closure traversal windows are always reevaluated. These limits bound local processing; they are not a physical-device latency guarantee or a globally optimal k-shortest-path algorithm.

Polygons are two-dimensional and do not encode travel direction or bridge identity. A polygon may conservatively remove both carriageways or the flyover above a closure. Human review and visible unavailable states remain essential. An announced end supports a conditional reopening assumption; a later still-active notice should revise its event end to unknown.

## Integration

Import `graphStatus` (alias `getGraphStatus`), `planJourney` and `searchPlaces` from `server/routing/index.ts`. `graphStatus` is synchronous and describes the local extract only. `planJourney` and `searchPlaces` return promises and must be awaited. A missing local graph reports `ready:false` but Pakistan journeys can still use the online adapter; failures never produce straight-line routes. Search combines local landmark/road names with Pakistan-filtered Photon results. `PlanResponse.source` distinguishes `local-osm` and `osrm`.

The online search adapter bounds queue/fetch/response parsing to eight seconds, rejects oversized responses, spaces provider requests at least a second apart and coalesces equivalent in-flight/cached queries. A failed provider can return genuine local matches, but never fabricated place coordinates. It does not promise every business/address or exact Google-style ranking. Photon documents search-as-you-type, typo tolerance, OSM coverage and a public-demo availability/throttling limit in its [official repository](https://github.com/komoot/photon#demo-server); country and result fields follow its [API documentation](https://github.com/komoot/photon/blob/master/docs/api-v1.md).

Preset endpoints are road approaches, not a claim of building entrances. Primary OSM verification (base 14 October 2026, 18:22 UTC) identifies Faisal Mosque Road node [3269003185](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3269003185), PIMS Gate FR / Ibn-e-Sina Road node [189236486](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/189236486), Centaurus / Nazimuddin Road node [9108779906](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/9108779906), and Aabpara Market / Municipal Road node [10041114361](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/10041114361). No field verification of access or main entrances is implied. The earlier approximate PIMS and F9 labels were not used in the final presets.

The bundled central extract covers **[72.97, 33.60, 73.16, 33.75]** (west, south, east, north), with **99,777 car-road nodes and 203,828 directed edges** after filtering. Complete source ways can extend outside the query rectangle; only the local engine bounds endpoint selection to this rectangle. The public planner uses the online adapter for other Pakistan endpoints. It includes the one mapped restriction relation returned for this extent; absence of an OSM restriction does not establish that every physical turn is legal. Import and runtime validation cannot replace local signs.

## Validation and limits

Meaningful regression coverage includes topology/access/one-way rules, real imported roads, supported turn restrictions, closure intervals, unknown expiry, blocked endpoints, disconnected crossings, diverse alternatives, ordered stops, unchanged reported times under search penalties, deadline expiry and online-provider/revision failures. Synthetic graphs appear only in tests, never as runtime routing fallbacks. See [implementation status](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) for executed checks.

Two-dimensional polygons do not encode bridge identity, travel direction or individual lanes. OSM access can be incomplete/outdated; a mapped street does not prove current physical access. Public online services can time out, restrict use or omit a legal detour. Return unavailable states rather than inventing geometry, traffic or clearance.
